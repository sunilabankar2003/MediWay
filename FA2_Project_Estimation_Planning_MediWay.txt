1. PROJECT DEFINITION

Title:
MediWay - Online Pharmacy Management System

Problem Statement:
Customers need a simple and reliable way to search medicines, add medicines to a
cart, upload prescriptions where required, complete payment, and receive order or
billing information. Manual pharmacy ordering is slower and makes tracking
prescription, payment and order status difficult. MediWay solves this by providing
a web-based medicine ordering and pharmacy management system.

Measurable Objectives:
1. Allow registered users to sign up, log in, and manage their profile.
2. Allow users to search medicines by name or company.
3. Display medicine details such as name, company, size, price, prescription need,
   and safety warning.
4. Allow users to add medicines to cart and update quantity.
5. Automatically calculate cart total and order amount.
6. Support prescription upload for prescription-required medicines.
7. Support online payment/order processing.
8. Maintain order status such as pending, approved, rejected, shipped, and delivered.
9. Generate billing information and invoice details.
10. Provide admin-side medicine, order, and billing management.

Target Users:
1. Patients/customers who want to order medicines online.
2. Pharmacy admin/staff who manage medicines, orders, prescriptions, and bills.
3. Project evaluators/testing users who review the application workflow.

3. COCOMO ESTIMATION

moderate complexity. The project uses known technologies such as Django, SQLite,

MediWay is estimated as a small Organic software project. Function Point Analysis
gives 75 Unadjusted Function Points. Using the COCOMO Organic model, the total
effort is approximately 7.54 Person-Months. CPM scheduling gives a project

32.17 days. This shows that schedule buffer is important, especially for payment
integration, prescription upload, testing, and final documentation.
